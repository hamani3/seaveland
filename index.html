Bulletin

Standing notices

N/01

Every document comes from the PresidentStanding

Passports and licences are applied for, not taken. Nothing is valid until the President has seen it and said so, and every document carries the number of the passport that approved it.

N/02

Titles are honoured once issuedStanding

A title written on a passport is a joke and is also, from the moment of issue, technically real. The Office does not consider these positions to be in conflict.

N/03

The pond is under constructionWorks

Not for much longer. Licences naming the pond are approved now and valid from the day it holds water, which is how most things here work.

N/04

The violations register is publicStanding

Anyone may look up any number. Entries are made by the President alone, disputed at the counter, and occasionally wiped for reasons the Office declines to give.

N/05

Bylaws and traditions — compilation in progressPending

The rules, the traditions, the banned things and the things that always happen are being written down as they are remembered. This notice will be replaced by the list.

N/06

The location of Seaveland is not publishedStanding

Not on this site, not on the passport, not at the counter. If you have to ask where it is, you were not going to be there anyway.

Seaveland is not a country and this is not a government. The passports and licences here are novelty documents, generated in your browser, valid nowhere, and recognised by nobody except the people who already know what Seaveland is.

Conditions are modelled from local climate records for the date and hour, not measured at the property. Documents and the register are held in this browser only — nothing is sent to a server, and a code or a link is the only way one device tells another anything.
